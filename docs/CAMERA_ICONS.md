# Camera icons for NotchShot

Created using the **built-in image_gen tool** (three separate generations). The original generated PNGs have genuine transparency; each is 1619 pixels wide and 971–972 pixels high. The app renders them at 90 × 54 points inside compact camera tiles. The illustrations are stylized depictions rather than exact product photographs.

## Online references

The visual direction was informed by lens-facing images and camera information found through web search, including Fujifilm's own product references:

- [X-T3 product page](https://www.fujifilm.com.hk/m/products/digital_cameras/x/fujifilm_x_t3/)
- [X100S front product view](https://www.fujifilm.com.hk/products/digital_cameras/x/fujifilm_x100s/product_views/)
- [FinePix F11 product archive](https://www.fujifilm-x.com/global/products/cameras/finepix-f11/)
- [FinePix F11 original press release](https://www.fujifilm.com.hk/news/cp/FinePix_F11.pdf)

The generated artwork is bundled locally; no product photographs are redistributed. Camera marks identify the corresponding shutter recordings. Generated files are preserved in this folder and included in the app's Resources.
